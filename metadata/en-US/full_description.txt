MinLauncher is a minimal, ad-free Android home screen launcher, with all promotional content and network access removed.

MinLauncher collects no analytics or telemetry and cannot access the network: it does not declare the INTERNET permission. Its optional screen-time and double-tap-to-lock features are processed entirely on the device.

It requires Android 11 or later and removes upstream promotional, remote-wallpaper, and legacy compatibility code found in the original project.
